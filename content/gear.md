This is the stuff I wear or use on a daily basis. 

* Smartwatch: I am wearing the [Casio G-Shock GBD-200](https://www.casio.com/intl/watches/gshock/product.GBD-200-1/) since 2025. Before that, I had the Amazfit Bip and the Amazfit GTS. With the last one I was missing a longer battery life, and when I found the Casio I knew it was perfect for me. You have to take the compromise of a color-less display and very limited smart features, but you get a long lasting battery. I was looking for a watch that could display seconds, alert about specific notifications and that didn't need a recharge every month. So far, the battery lasts for 12 months.
